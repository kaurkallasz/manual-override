Drag the sketch nozzle to adjust elbow J3, or drag the sketch elbow to adjust shoulder J2. The white sketch follows your selected target immediately, and the sliders stay synchronized with sketch drags. The red outline and slider indicators follow the arm's measured position as it moves. Arrow buttons beside the base controls and at each slider end change the target by 1 degree per press, or 0.1 degree with Fine tune on. Fine tune also reduces sketch movement tenfold and zooms each slider to one tenth of its range around the selected target, with 0.05 degree drag steps. Toggle off for the full range; toggle on again to center a new zoom. Red arrows at an edge mean the actual angle is outside the zoomed range.

Tap a saved preset to recall its height. Hold for 3 seconds to save or replace the selected Z height on this phone.

In Targeting, tap to preview a destination or drag the green bullseye, then press Go; Fine tune reduces drag movement to one tenth. Use two fingers to pan and zoom. In XYZ, sliders select map coordinates; red markers show actual arm position. Green bullseye and dashed cross lines show your XY target; thin red bullseye and dashed cross lines show actual XY. The right vertical Y slider increases downward; the left height slider increases upward. Arrows move X and Y by 10 map units, and height by 1 unit (1 mm in physical play), or 1 map unit and 0.1 height unit with Fine tune enabled. Height stays available in Targeting and while Cue is stopped. Orange means XY aligned; height is separate. Virtual heights range from 0 to 400. Physical play uses calibrated camera coordinates, millimeter heights, and the displayed red reach limits. Fine tune zooms to one tenth of the range with 0.1 unit slider steps.
