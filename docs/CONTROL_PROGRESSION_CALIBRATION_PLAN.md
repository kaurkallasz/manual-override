# Mobile control progression and calibration approval plan

**Status:** Approved by the user's instruction to implement; implemented in the working tree. See `CONTROL_PROGRESSION_IMPLEMENTATION.md` for delivery and verification. Physical calibration accuracy and human balance require supervised measurements.
**Prepared:** 30 September 2026.
**Requested executor after approval:** GPT-6.1 Sol, Extra high reasoning (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `xhigh`).
**Scope:** Complete the Green and Purple mobile experience across controls, transitions, calibration, LTZ Score, persistent players, statistics, and level-specific balancing.

The implementation should connect and finish the substantial existing functionality rather than replace it. The player journey will be **Joint → XYZ → six-point Calibration → Targeting → Cue autonomy**, with LTZ Score providing results, purchases, statistics, and unlock screens between games. The user confirmed that calibration follows Level 2 XYZ and precedes Level 3 Targeting, and that **1,500 seconds means one first-time game run**, not the entire progression campaign.

**Co-op timing:** Both players play and advance together. The normal upgrade screen lasts **30 seconds on one shared timer**. Immediately after the third qualifying Level 2 XYZ win is saved, automatically enter the calibration sequence with **Green first, then Purple**. Calibration uses the unified playfield **one player at a time**; the other player stays on the upgrade screen with a waiting message. That calibration wait may extend beyond 30 seconds.

**Calibration persistence:** Keep each arm's last validated calibration across games and restarts, and use it to enforce bounds from Joint and XYZ onward. A valid saved rig calibration does not exempt a player from the required post-XYZ calibration and click-to-move lesson.

**Required current tier:** Each player stays on their current control tier until three qualifying wins advance them to the next tier. Players cannot choose an earlier tier or skip ahead. Persist the current tier across games, losses, reconnects, and later visits. After reaching Cue, continue playing Cue. Gamemaster can temporarily open other tiers for practice without changing the player's saved progression.

**Difficulty evidence update:** The user requires balancing from the shortening game times with each new control system, **without using a median**. The intended evidence is the four-day period around 20 August, provisionally searched as 20–23 August 2026 with nearby dates included. The current `pickup-game/pickup-log.csv` contains June results, not those August games. Use the original method-labelled August results to compare Joint, XYZ, Targeting, and Cue separately, then increase both counts and release frequency. The earlier pooled-time baseline, derived placement budget, and numerical tier schedules are withdrawn.

### Contracts, transaction boundaries and lifecycle

1. **Document before wiring.** Existing contracts include `photon.game` v2, `photon.progress` v1, `photon.board.runtime`/`placement` v1, `hhh.cal2.projection` v1 and `hhh.relay.arms` v1. Proposed additions are Auto Pickup session, measurement and safety descriptors, a Playfield display-session contract, and a nested Game journey descriptor, each with a named integer version, status, bounded JSON values, revision and relevant source timestamps. They are new work, not callable interfaces today. Validate enabled discovery, contract name, exact integer type/version, status, freshness and shape before mutations; missing or incompatible prerequisites are unavailable.
2. **Version behavioral changes explicitly.** Mandatory tiers and reserved ready-rosters change Progress's selectable-control/policy semantics: publish the revised `photon.progress` v2 contract and update Game/ProgressLink, Relay, mobile facade, both LTZ Score clients and their fixtures together. Migrate stored/backup schema independently; preserve historical records. Do not silently relabel old `selected_control` behavior or fall back to a lower privilege model. Game's journey can be an additive separately named descriptor within its v2 output; preserve Board and Level's existing contracts unless their actual semantics change.
3. **Keep commits local.** Progress atomically commits the result, rewards, next tier and pending lesson milestone. Game durably records the intermission/queue referencing that milestone, with an idempotent reconciliation step before the result outbox is discarded. Auto Pickup atomically stores each draft and committed rig revision; Game validates the guided exercise from existing evidence and submits a unique completion receipt to Progress. Only after those acknowledgements and stopped-motion confirmation may Game request the next calibration turn. Persist intent/receipts in each owner's own store and retry public operations; no cross-module file access or assumed distributed transaction.
4. **Close purchases and freeze once.** At the shared upgrade boundary Game asks Progress to close purchases and reserve the ready roster/revisions through a public atomic operation. A purchase committed first is included; a later one is rejected. `begin_attempt` remains the sole transaction that freezes participants, tiers and loadouts for a scored run; it verifies that reservation and rechecks the two-player roster. Game carries the resulting snapshot as run metadata, never a separately editable inventory. A cancelled readiness reservation has an explicit release/recovery policy and never restarts the countdown implicitly.
5. **Separate sequence from reservation.** Game persists whose lesson turn is due; Auto Pickup grants the one authoritative whole-field reservation with an epoch/revision. Relay, calibration writes and Playfield mutations enforce that same reservation through public outputs. Restart invalidates live motion grants; a saved draft/queue is resumable only after fresh validation and never reconnects or moves hardware automatically. Handoff keeps the previous reservation until motion is confirmed stopped. Stop/cancel remains usable when a dependent owner is unavailable.
6. **Avoid call cycles and lock coupling.** Board's sampler already calls Auto Pickup's pure projection. Auto Pickup's measurement operation therefore obtains fresh tag data through Webcam → Camera Calibration and arm data through Relay's read-only contract, without calling Board back during projection. The safety/reservation descriptor is a copied cached value and never calls Game, Board or Relay. Game reads Board's existing cached relationship to assess the exercise. Release owner locks before external calls, then compare revisions before committing; do not perform blocking device/file work on the simulation tick or hold Progress transactions across sibling calls.
7. **Distinguish control mode from motion primitives.** A Targeting action still executes Cartesian motion and permits its defined Z/pump controls; Cue needs its own validated internal steps. Bind top-level mode and allowed actions to a server-issued session. Do not reject legitimate primitives merely because they are shared with an earlier tier, or authorize them from a browser's `ltz_action` label alone. Tutorial and Gamemaster grants are explicit temporary scopes. Existing relay motion delivery remains the only physical execution path.
8. **Preserve lifecycle and presentation ownership.** New timers, samplers, persistence workers and stream resources start in their owner's `hub_init` and stop/join in `hub_stop`. Reuse the existing Game/Board workers and SSE helper where possible. Browsers display server deadlines; Game never fits calibration models, handles image bytes or parses authored map files. Game settings UI remains Game-owned; Y links/forwards rather than duplicating its form or storage. Historical CSV/score analysis is an offline report over an explicit export, never a runtime sibling-file dependency.

## Player journey and transition screens

Keep gameplay tier, map, calibration lesson, and screen state separate. Calibration is an interlude, not a fifth combat tier, and never multiplies orc counts or grants kill credits.

1. **Player selection:** a searchable saved-name dropdown with “Create new player.” Show selected name, team, saved credits, current required control, and wins remaining until the next tier. Use stable player IDs; do not use names as storage keys. Handle duplicate names explicitly and prevent one profile occupying both teams simultaneously.
2. **LTZ Score and loadout:** show owned upgrades, prices, mastery pips, statistics, and history beneath the upgrade table. Initial profile setup/history browsing is available in the lobby; the in-game upgrade phase follows the shared 30-second rule below. Returning players resume their saved current tier or pending calibration lesson. Show progression status rather than a control selector: earlier tiers completed, current tier active, future tiers locked.
3. **Control introduction:** explain the player's assigned current control in a short mobile overlay, with a small diagram/animation using existing assets. Any player-facing practice entry uses that same tier and awards no progression; other-tier practice is available only through Gamemaster testing. Return to LTZ Score without changing tier.
4. **Ready screen:** show both participants, assigned controls, frozen loadouts, physical readiness, practice/scored status, preset name/revision, and scheduled orcs. Both players must be present and ready; Game validates the complete two-player roster before a normal co-op Start. Initial readiness is explicit. Between runs, readiness remains valid only while the roster, connection, and hardware checks remain valid; the upgrade timer never bypasses those checks.
5. **Playing:** use the existing shared mobile controls. Hide purchases/profile switching and prevent them on the server during the active attempt.
6. **Saving result:** end motion through the existing lifecycle and finalize the shared attempt. Display “Saving result” until Progress confirms the durable result; reconnect/retry must not award twice.
7. **Results and unlock:** once the result is durably saved, start one shared 30-second countdown. Normally both players see upgrades with shared kills, outcome, active time, credits added, balances, mastery pips, and any new control card. **At the third Level 2 win, route Green directly into calibration and Purple directly to upgrades with the waiting message.** Green does not first visit or dismiss a results/upgrade screen. A compact milestone message may appear within calibration without delaying entry. Reuse LTZ Score's visual language and reveal each unlock once without extending or restarting the timer.
8. **Between games:** offer upgrades and history during the ordinary shared window; the next run automatically uses each player's current tier. At 30 seconds, both leave upgrades together unless calibration is active or queued. The Level 2 milestone automatically starts **Green → Purple** calibration; no **Calibrate for Targeting** button or countdown expiry is required to enter it. After Green completes calibration and the guided exercise, automatically take Purple from upgrades into calibration and return Green to upgrades with **Waiting for Purple to finish calibration**. Once Purple finishes and the original shared timer has expired, both advance together to their assigned control introduction/ready state: Targeting for profiles that just completed XYZ. Do not add another 30-second phase after calibration. After Targeting mastery, automatically assign Cue; after Cue mastery, continue at Cue during subsequent runs.

Progress owns earned transitions; Game owns active run/readiness/intermission/calibration-session state. The browser renders those facts and retains only harmless presentation preferences. Dismissing a screen or refreshing cannot skip a required lesson, restart the 30 seconds, or unlock a control. Persist completion and pending progression facts so returning players can continue after a restart.

Mixed-tier co-op uses the higher of the two assigned current tiers to determine the shared battlefield's preset/count factor; each profile earns mastery only for its own frozen tier. Show the resulting preset before both players are ready. A partner's progress never promotes, resets, or downgrades the other profile. Different mastery states do not allow independent level starts or reverse the calibration order. A new Level 2 milestone enters the shared Green → Purple sequence even when the pair's saved progress differs. An already-completed player's turn revalidates the rig without resetting earned lesson completion; a player who has not yet earned the milestone receives practice access only, without a free tier unlock. Neither starts combat while the shared field is reserved for calibration.

### Mandatory tier progression

Persist a monotonic `current_control_tier` per player, with lesson/progression stage stored separately. A new profile starts at Joint. Each durable third qualifying win advances exactly once: **Joint → XYZ → Targeting (calibration required) → Cue**. Wins need not be consecutive. Defeat, abort, game reset, reconnect, switching teams, and returning another day preserve the tier and earned wins. Freeze the participant's tier at run start and apply the result to that tier before committing the advancement; duplicate finalization cannot advance twice.

At the third XYZ win, persist Tier 3 with a pending required lesson and immediately enter the shared Green → Purple sequence. Targeting combat remains blocked until the lesson and readiness checks pass. Closing or interrupting the lesson resumes that pending stage; it cannot restore XYZ gameplay or skip calibration. Tutorial-only movement permissions remain scoped to the active calibration turn and do not change the assigned combat tier. At Cue, three wins mark mastery, and Cue remains the current tier for all further scored play.

Normal mobile, LTZ Score, Start, and motion APIs must enforce the assigned tier server-side. Reject lower-tier requests, premature higher-tier requests, stale mode commands, and forged client selections. Removing the mode selector alone is insufficient. Gamemaster may grant a temporary, clearly labelled practice tier; ending that session restores the saved current tier or pending lesson, with no practice rewards or mastery changes.

For existing profiles, derive the current tier from the highest earned tier supported by saved mastery/unlock records. Preserve all scores, purchases, wins, and history. A previously selected lower control is not permission to remain below the earned tier. Advanced profiles without a lesson record retain their earned tier but enter the required lesson before combat. If progression records are inconsistent, surface a recovery state rather than silently lowering the tier or granting advancement.

### Shared countdown and waiting behavior

Game publishes one intermission ID, phase, upgrade deadline, calibration queue/active player, per-player completion, and transition revision to both phones through the existing SSE path. The deadline starts once after result finalization; refresh, reconnect, unlock animation, or one phone's local clock cannot reset it. Keep this intermission outside active-run duration and combat/spawn clocks.

| Shared state | Active player's phone | Other player's phone | Transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary upgrades | Upgrades and the shared 30-second countdown | Same | At expiry, both enter the next ready/control screen together. |
| First calibration turn — Green | Green enters calibration immediately after the third Level 2 win is finalized | Purple on upgrades: **Waiting for Green to finish calibration. Your turn is next.** | After Green's validated completion and stopped motion, automatically enter Purple's calibration turn. |
| Second calibration turn — Purple | Purple enters calibration automatically | Green on upgrades: **Calibration complete. Waiting for Purple to finish calibration.** | When Purple completes, advance both if the original timer has expired. |
| Calibration finishes before the deadline | Both return to upgrades for the remaining shared time | Same | Advance at the original deadline; no reset or extra phase. |
| Calibration exceeds the deadline | Continue calibration; no 30-second calibration timeout | Remain on upgrades with the waiting message | Wait for all required calibration to finish, then advance both together. |

At the third Level 2 win, calibration begins immediately while the shared 30-second timer starts in parallel. The timer is not a prerequisite, a separate allowance per player, or a calibration timeout. Purple can browse statistics/history and buy upgrades while Green calibrates; Green gets that opportunity while Purple calibrates. At the shared transition to ready, close purchases and reserve both profile revisions atomically in Progress: purchases accepted before that boundary are included; later requests are rejected clearly. Freeze the verified roster/loadouts once through Progress's `begin_attempt` at Start, as specified in the architecture section. Game never creates a competing purchase or inventory store.

If a connection, save, or hardware check prevents continuing after the ordinary countdown, display a clear readiness/recovery state for the pair. Do not start one player's game alone or turn a fault into a fresh upgrade countdown. During a calibration interruption, the partner remains on upgrades with an interrupted/waiting message. A completed queue advances both screens together, but does not automatically move either robot or start a cue.

“Continue” means resume the saved current tier or pending lesson and start another run at that tier. This scope retains current crash recovery of banked scores; it does not introduce restoration of in-flight enemies or robot motion.
